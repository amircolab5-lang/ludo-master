📜 Official Ludo Rules

1. Roll 6 to Open: A pawn moves from the yard onto the starting tile only when a 6 is rolled.

2. Bonus Turns: Rolling a 6 or cutting an opponent's pawn grants an extra bonus roll.

3. Cutting Enemy: Landing on an opponent's pawn on any normal track tile captures it and returns it to their yard.

4. Safe Stars: The 8 star/globe tiles are safe zones where pawns can never be cut.

5. Three 6s Rule: Rolling three consecutive sixes cancels the turn, passing play to the next opponent.

6. Exact Home Entry: An exact roll is required for a pawn to enter the Home triangle.
